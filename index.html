        What each skill did

          Safety rules

          • No order goes anywhere. The pipeline ends at a proposal.
          • Paper trading only. A live account would need three separate yeses: the risk check, a typed yes for each order, and the broker's own approval card.
          • 10% stays in cash, and no stock may exceed 25% of the budget.
          • A 13F shows only long US stocks, as of a quarter end, filed up to 45 days later. It is a firm's filing, not Warren Buffett's personal trades.

          Education only. Not financial advice. This is a simulation with pretend money. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and past results do not predict future ones. Built with the open-source Trader skill.
